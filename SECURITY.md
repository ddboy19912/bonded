# Security

Bonded's contracts run on testnets, with test tokens. We take every security report seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately through GitHub: the repository's **Security** tab, then **Report a vulnerability**. Or email security@bondedhq.xyz. Don't open a public issue for a vulnerability.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it. We'll acknowledge it within a few days and tell you what we'll do.

## Attacks on AI agents

Attacks that trick an agent into breaking its mandate are what Bonded's bounties pay for. Submit those through the bounty board instead ([docs/BOUNTIES.md](docs/BOUNTIES.md)). The [disclosure policy](docs/disclosures/README.md) explains how findings about third-party agents are handled.

## What has been checked

The internal security review, with its findings and their status, is [docs/AUDIT.md](docs/AUDIT.md). It is an in-house review, not an external audit. Known open items are listed there.
